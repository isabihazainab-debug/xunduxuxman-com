FAQ

Before you book.

How do I book?

WhatsApp is fastest — we usually reply within minutes. You can also call +92 304 7189919. Bridal dates should be locked 2–4 weeks ahead, and 6–8 weeks in peak wedding season.

Is a trial included?

Yes, in every package above Nikkah. The trial is booked on a weekday so we can take our time and photograph the look for the day.

What products do you use?

Imported, sealed products from premium global brands, plus Guinot for facials. Brushes and tools are sterilised between every client. If you have sensitive skin, tell us at booking and we patch-test at the trial.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes, across Punjab. Travel, stay and an early-call fee are quoted separately once we know the venue and timings.

What are your hours?

Open daily, 10 AM – 7 PM. Bridal mornings start earlier by arrangement.

Do I need a deposit?

Bridal dates are held with an advance. It goes toward your final bill and secures the date against other enquiries.
